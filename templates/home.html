A SIMPLE JOURNEY OF SHARING

How FoodFlow Works

From surplus food to someone's table, before it expires.

01

Post Surplus Food

Donors list suitable surplus food with its quantity, expiry time and pickup location.

02

Local Alerts

Matching verified recipients receive in-app alerts based on the donation's area and city.

03

Claim a Portion

Verified recipients review available food, claim the quantity they need and receive a pickup code.

04

Arrive for Pickup

Recipients visit the listed pickup location before their deadline, with their pickup code ready.

05

Verify & Complete

The donor enters the recipient's code to confirm the pickup and mark the claim as completed.

SHARE FOOD. SHARE KINDNESS.

Who can be a donor?

A restaurant with extra prepared meals. A grocery shop with surplus produce. A household with more food than it needs. FoodFlow gives donors a way to share suitable, unexpired food with verified recipients instead of letting it go to waste.

Restaurants & Food Businesses

Share suitable extra meals with clear preparation, quantity and expiry details.

Grocers & Local Shops

Give surplus fruits, vegetables and other suitable food a chance to be used.

Households & Individuals

Turn suitable extra food into a thoughtful contribution to your community.

A thoughtful donation starts with clear details.

Describe your food accurately, mention the available quantity and expiry time, and provide a clear pickup location. When the recipient arrives, verify their pickup code to complete the handover.

GOOD FOOD. SHARED WITH PURPOSE.

Who Can Be A Recipient?

FoodFlow supports individuals seeking food for themselves and organisations collecting food for others. NGOs, charities and shelters can connect with donors to support the communities they serve. Recipient accounts must be verified before they can claim available food.

Individuals

Find suitable food donations and claim the quantity you need.

NGOs & Charities

Collect suitable donations to support people in the communities you serve.

Shelters & Community Support

Connect with food donors and arrange collection for those you support.

Collect with care. Share responsibly.

Check the description, expiry time and pickup location before claiming. Claim only what you can collect and use, arrive before the food expires, and keep your pickup code ready to show the donor.
